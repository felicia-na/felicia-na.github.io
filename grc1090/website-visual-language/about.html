Morro Tea began with a simple idea: create a welcoming place where people could slow down, enjoy a thoughtfully made drink, and feel connected to Morro Bay. Inspired by the town’s shoreline, sea life, and iconic Morro Rock, the cafe brings a relaxed coastal spirit together with a love of Asian-inspired tea traditions. What started as a vision for a neighborhood tea shop grew into a gathering place for anyone looking for a comforting cup, a refreshing pick-me-up, or a quiet moment by the coast.

At the heart of Morro Tea is a commitment to quality. Each drink is made with care, from smooth matcha and fragrant loose-leaf teas to balanced milk teas and carefully prepared lattes. The goal is to let the ingredients shine, whether a guest chooses a bright, fruit-forward tea, a creamy specialty drink, or a warm cup on a cool coastal morning. Morro Tea wants every sip to feel considered and satisfying, with flavors that invite guests to return and discover something new.

Morro Tea welcomes both the people who call Morro Bay home and the visitors who come to experience its beauty. Locals can make the cafe part of their everyday routine, while travelers can find a friendly stop to rest and enjoy a taste inspired by the coast. The shop hopes to bring people together across generations and backgrounds, creating an easygoing space to meet a friend, take a break after a walk, or carry a favorite drink along the waterfront.

Because Morro Tea's identity is rooted in the natural beauty of the area, caring for that environment is part of its purpose. The cafe donates a portion of its profits to charities and local organizations that help preserve the land, protect wildlife, and care for the coastal habitats that make Morro Bay special. With every cup, Morro Tea aims to support not only the people who live and visit here, but also the shoreline and wildlife that give this community its character.
