Introduction

Morro Tea

Morro Tea is an Asian inspired specialty drink and tea cafe based in Morro Bay. The cafe is known for its milk teas, matcha, and beachy theme. Customers can quickly order online or visit the aesthic cafe to take in the scenic views of Morro Bay as they sip on their drink. The mascot is a sea otter because they are a native species in Morro Bay.

Target audience

Customers who are looking to buy their drinks online or new visitors of Morro Bay wanting to know what the business is all about.

Their primary tasks are to browse over the drink choices, place orders, and learn more about the cafe.
